## Presentation fonts

**Always use these two fonts for slides. No exceptions.**

| Role | Font | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| All titles and headers | Verdana Bold | Verdana has no light variant; always Bold |
| All body text | Avenir | Use Regular (35) or Medium (55) |

---

## Presentation size scale

| Level | Font | Size | Weight | Use |
|-------|------|------|--------|-----|
| Slide title | Verdana | 36–44pt | Bold | Main slide heading |
| Section header | Verdana | 24–28pt | Bold | Sub-headings, block labels |
| Body large | Avenir | 18–20pt | Regular/Medium | Primary content |
| Body | Avenir | 14–16pt | Regular | Standard content, bullets |
| Caption | Avenir | 10–12pt | Regular | Source notes, footnotes |

**Rules:**
- Maximum 3 different sizes on a single slide
- Title is always the largest text on the slide
- Do not use Arial, Calibri, or any other system default font
